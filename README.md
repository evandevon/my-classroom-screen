# My Classroom Screen

A free classroom display for the front-of-room screen, made for touch smartboards. Add widgets — timers, a noise meter, a name picker, a whiteboard and more — then move and resize them anywhere on the screen. Make as many screens as you like, one for each routine, lesson or class.

Made by teachers, for teachers. No accounts and no ads, and it works offline. Everything is saved in the browser on your own device.

**Live site:** https://evandevon.github.io/my-classroom-screen/

## Widgets

The widget bar at the bottom has four groups. Tap one to see its widgets.

### ⏰ Time & weather

- **⏳ Timer** — ring, white→red circle, white→red rectangle, hourglass, bar or numbers; quick-time buttons; a label and end sound.
- **⏱️ Stopwatch** with laps.
- **🔂 Interval timer** — work/rest rounds with a get-ready, 3-2-1 beeps and presets like Tabata; rings on time in a background tab.
- **🕒 Clock** — 8 styles: analogue/digital, teaching clock (past/to, time in words, set-a-time and quiz), Roman numerals, word clock, sundial with a draggable sun, candle, water and incense clocks.
- **⏰ Alarms** — school bells with a pack-up warning beforehand, a choice of days and sounds.
- **🗓️ Activity timeline** — coloured sessions with icons, a now/next countdown, plans for each weekday, type-in quick entry.
- **📅 Calendar** — tap a day to add events, repeats, "coming up" countdowns, and school term and week numbers for every Australian state and territory and New Zealand.
- **🔜 Countdown** — "12 sleeps until the excursion!" in days, sleeps, school days or weeks, optionally skipping the holidays.
- **🌦️ Weather** — now, the next 6 hours, 5 days, UV sun-safety tips, rain alerts, °C/°F. Free data from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/); only the rough place is sent.
- **☀️ Weather today** — one big picture of the day: high/low, the school-hours forecast, and what to bring (hat, umbrella, jumper, water).

### 🧑‍🏫 Class

- **🔊 Noise meter** — bouncy balls, a level bar or an emoji face, with a too-loud warning.
- **🚦 Traffic light** with your own labels.
- **🤫 Work symbols** — voice levels and how to work.
- **➡️ Now / Next** visual schedule.
- **🎡 Name picker** — spinning wheel or flash card, with no repeats.
- **👥 Group maker** — by number of groups or group size, keeping apart students who shouldn't work together.
- **🔢 Tally** — count interruptions, great answers… for the class or each student.
- **✅ Progress** — an optional heading, then a row of tick boxes for each student in the class, so children can mark how far through a task they are. + and − set the number of boxes; rows spread into columns to fit; ticks are kept for each class.
- **🏆 Scoreboard** — 2–8 teams, or a row for each student.
- **🔔 Quiz buzzer** — 2–8 teams; the first touch wins and only its sound plays; shows 2nd and 3rd and how far behind; keyboard keys, so Makey Makey or Arduino buttons work.

### 🖼️ Words & media

- **📝 Text** — bold, lists, sizes, colours.
- **✅ List / checklist**, numbered, or a turn order (games, speakers).
- **✏️ Whiteboard**
  - Pens, 3 highlighters, and straight lines, arrows, dashed and dotted lines and number lines that snap together.
  - Move line ends and corners; the 📐 Straighten switch turns hand-drawn "straight" lines straight.
  - Eraser and undo (which also undoes Clear), with a size circle under the mouse; grid, lined or chalkboard.
  - **🖼 Pictures to draw over:** paste one (tap the whiteboard, then Ctrl+V), drop one on it, or choose a file. Drag a picture to move it, drag a corner to resize it, and use 🗑 to remove it. Pens draw over pictures, and the eraser leaves them alone.
  - Several fingers can draw at once.
  - ⏺ Record a session with optional voice, play it back at ½–4× with a loop, and save it as an MP4 quickly, even in a background tab.
- **🖼️ Image** — upload, paste or link.
- **🎴 Random picture** — choose a folder of pictures; no repeats, changes by itself, captions, a blurry "mystery" reveal. Pictures stay in this browser.
- **🎬 Video / Web** — YouTube, Google Drive files and folders, Slides, Docs, Vimeo, Canva, Spotify, websites; a pop-up window for sites that block embedding; plain-words messages when a link is wrong, a YouTube video can't be embedded, or a site is blocked or slow, plus a ❓ *Can't see it?* button.
- **🎵 Audio** — upload, an MP3 link, Drive, YouTube or Spotify, plus the 🎧 Sound library (below).
- **📷 Webcam / document camera** — flip, rotate, zoom, freeze, full-resolution photos and ⏺ recorded clips; each opens in its own widget with a ⬇ download button.
- **🔁 Video loop** — each webcam recording loops in its own widget (Step 1, Step 2…) at normal, half or quarter speed with a download; or loop any video file.
- **🔳 QR code** (works offline).
- **➡️ Go to screen** — a big button that opens another of your screens. Put one on each screen to step through a lesson. It finds the screen by name after a file or link.

### 🎲 Maths & chance

- **🧮 Calculator** (works safely without `eval`).
- **🗳️ Poll** — a question with tappable emoji or word answers; results can stay hidden until revealed.
- **🎲 Dice** — 1–6 dice, any number of sides from 2.
- **🌀 Spinner** — equal or bigger sections ("Red ×3"), with a running tally compared with what we'd expect, for chance experiments, choices and brain breaks.
- **🪙 Coin flip** — flip 1–10 coins, with a heads/tails tally and percentages.
- **📚 Story dice** — roll picture dice and tell a story that uses every picture; tap a die to keep it.

## Screens

- **☰ menu:** make, open, rename, duplicate, delete and drag screens into order. Using a screen never moves it; new screens go at the end.
- **The menu is in colour-coded cards:** 🗂️ My screens (blue), 🖼️ This screen (purple), 💾 Save & share (green), 👥 Classes (orange), ⚙️ Options (grey) and ❓ Help (pink). A row of shortcuts at the top stays in place as you scroll: tap one to jump to that card, and the one you're in lights up.
- **Starting points:** start blank or from a ready-made screen. The choices are Morning routine, Group work, Quiet reading, Maths lesson, Test conditions, Writing task, Science investigation, Stations / rotations, Brain break, Pack up and Relief teacher day.
- **Quick switcher:** tap the screen's name at the top.
  - With up to 8 screens it shows them all.
  - With more, it shows the screens in this screen's group (or the screens not in a group) in their usual order, then up to four **Recently used** from elsewhere.
  - **☰ All screens…** opens the full list.
- **Pause screen** (☰ → This screen) stops everything so you can carry on tomorrow. Timers and stopwatches keep their time; alarms, cameras, microphones and videos switch off.
- **📸 Save a picture (JPG or PDF)** (☰ → This screen) takes one picture of the screen without the menu and widget bar.
  - The browser asks to share the tab; choose *Share*. It grabs one frame and stops, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  - Browsers that can't do this (e.g. iPad Safari) are pointed to the screenshot keys.

### Groups of screens

- **Making groups:** ☰ → **+ New group** (e.g. *4K*, *Science*, *Routines*). Each group gets its own colour; tap 🎨 to choose one of eight. ✎ renames a group; 🗑 deletes the group but keeps its screens.
- **Putting a screen in a group:**
  - drag its ⋮⋮ handle into the group (or onto a closed group's heading);
  - or use its **📁** button, which is handy on a smartboard.
  - Groups are one level only: a screen is in one group or none.
- **Taking a screen out of a group:** drag it to the **No group** area, which appears while you drag, or choose *No group* with 📁.
- **While dragging:** a copy of the screen follows your finger or mouse, a dashed gap shows where it will land, the group it will join lights up, and the menu scrolls if you drag near its top or bottom.
- **Moving groups:** drag a group's ⋮⋮ handle to move the whole group up or down, or focus the handle and use the arrow keys.
- **Opening and closing:** tap a group's heading to close or open it, and the site remembers. A closed group holding the screen you're on says *· here*.
- **New screens** go into the same group as the screen you were on. Saved files and backups bring their groups with them, joining a group of the same name if there is one.
- **Recently used:** once there are six or more screens, the last few you opened appear at the top of the menu.

### Undo and redo

- **↶ ↷ in the top bar, or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (Ctrl+Y):** undo anything you did on a screen.
  - That covers adding, moving, resizing or removing widgets, settings, scores, votes, typing and backgrounds, and deleting a screen.
  - Things that change by themselves (a running timer, weather updates, a slideshow) aren't undo steps.
- In a text box, Ctrl+Z undoes your typing as usual.

### More than one tab

- **Different screens in different tabs:** each tab can show a different screen. A new tab goes straight to your last screen, unless another tab is already using it; then it starts on a free screen and asks **Which screen in this tab?**
- **A screen is used in one tab at a time.** Opening it in a second tab moves it there.
  - The first tab stops running the screen: no timers, sounds, videos, camera or microphone, and no saving.
  - It shows **"…is being used in another tab"** with two buttons: **Use it here** (takes it back, with the other tab's latest changes) and **Show a different screen**.
  - Running timers carry on in the tab that takes over, so two tabs never overwrite each other or ring twice.
- **Class lists** changed in one tab update in the others straight away. Each tab keeps its own current class.
- **Completely separate sets of screens:** use two browser profiles (e.g. *School* and *Home*); each has its own storage.

## Classes and student lists (stay on this device)

- **☰ → Classes & student lists:** make a class, then type names or paste a column from a spreadsheet. You can also import or export CSV to move lists to another computer.
- **👥 button in the top bar:** choose the current class. It can also switch by itself when the Activity timeline's session name contains a class name.
- **Widgets that use names:**
  - The Name picker, Group maker, Tally (per student) and Scoreboard (a row for each student) use the current class's names, or their own typed list.
  - Progress always uses a class list, and keeps each class's ticks separately.
- **Every name in a class must be different**, or widgets like Progress would mix two students up. If a name is typed twice, the editor asks for a surname initial (e.g. *Sam B*); until then it saves the repeat as "Sam (2)". Imported files and shared classes are treated the same way.
- **🚫 Keep apart:** in a class's list, choose pairs of students who shouldn't be in the same group. The Group maker keeps them apart, and tells you if it can't (e.g. with too few groups).

## Saving, sharing and backups

Your screens **save themselves** in this browser as you go. To move, share or back them up, use ☰ → *Save & share*, which has three parts: **Keep a safe copy**, **Send a copy** and **Bring screens in**. Links and files are always **copies, not live links**: once sent, the screens on each computer change separately.

| | ⬇ Back up everything | 💾 Save to a file | 🔗 Send a copy as a link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good for | Keeping a safe copy | Sending screens to a colleague, or moving some to another computer | Sending one screen quickly |
| Screens | All of them | The ones you tick | The one showing |
| Class lists | All of them | The ones those screens use | The ones that screen uses, unless you untick the box |
| Screen groups | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Pictures, clips and sounds from this computer | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ (the share window warns you) |

- **Backups:** clearing the browser's data, a reset laptop or a new computer would lose your screens.
  - The menu shows when everything was last backed up to a file, with a gentle ⚠ after four weeks (or if never).
  - Keep the backup somewhere safe, like OneDrive. Saving every screen with *Save to a file* counts as a backup too.
  - The site also asks the browser not to clear its storage to free up space.
- **Reminders:** on the second day someone uses the site (with a few widgets made and no backup yet), a small card asks **once** whether to save a backup file. The end-of-term note offers one too, since laptops are often reset over the holidays.
- **Bringing screens in** always works the same way. Use ☰ → **Add screens from a file**, drag a `.classroom` file onto the screen, or open a share link.
  - A window lists the screens with tick boxes, marks any **you already have** (unticked), and shows which class lists come too.
  - Nothing changes until you press **Add**. Added screens go alongside yours.
  - **Same name as one of yours** (but different contents, e.g. an updated copy coming back from home): the row asks what to do.
    - **Keep both** (the default): type a name for the new copy (it suggests "Name (2)") and choose its group.
    - **Replace mine**: your screen gets the new contents but keeps its name, group and place in the list. **Undo** on the message puts it back. If that screen is open in another tab, that tab steps aside first so it can't save its old copy over the new one.
    - **Copy to my screens** from a preview asks the same thing.
  - A shared class with the same name as one of yours but different students is added as "Name (2)".
  - **Adding a backup** brings back every screen, class list and group (with its colour and whether it was closed).
- **👁 Preview** on any row opens that screen in a **new tab**, so your own screen keeps running, and the window you previewed from stays open to choose screens to add.
  - The bar says **"Looking at “…” — nothing here is saved automatically."** You can use the screen for real: timers, name picker, whiteboard.
  - **➕ Copy to my screens** copies it into your screens as it is now, including your changes; your other tab is told and its list updates. After that the button reads **⟳ Update my copy**, which updates the same copy rather than making another.
  - It stays a preview: later changes aren't saved unless you press *Update my copy*, and closing the tab with uncopied changes asks first.
  - **✕** hides the bar; **👁 Preview** at the top brings it back. **Other screens in the file** and **Close this tab** are on the bar too.
  - On phones and small tablets, or if the browser blocks new tabs, the preview opens in the same tab instead.
- **Class lists in shared screens:** only share with colleagues; it's up to you who gets the names. On the other computer they're added to that teacher's classes. A class with the same name and students isn't doubled up.
- **What's inside a file:** a `.classroom` file is a zip holding `classroom.json` (screens, class lists and groups) and a `files/` folder with every photo, clip, picture and sound at its real size. Rename it to `.zip` to look inside. Older `.json` files still open.
- **Tidying up:** files no screen uses any more (e.g. from a deleted widget) are removed automatically.

## Pasting and dropping pictures, videos and sounds

- **Paste a picture:** copy one anywhere (right-click → Copy image, or a screenshot), then press **Ctrl+V** (⌘V on a Mac) on the screen.
  - It becomes an Image widget, sized to the picture's shape. Several pictures at once each get their own widget.
  - A copied link to a picture (ending .jpg, .png, .gif…) works too.
  - If you tapped a whiteboard first, the picture goes onto the whiteboard instead.
- **Drag files onto the screen** from a folder (or a picture straight from a web page). Pictures become Image widgets, short videos become Video loops, and sound files become Audio widgets, right where you drop them. Dropping onto an existing Image widget or a whiteboard puts it there instead.
- **On a smartboard without a keyboard:** add an Image widget and tap **📋 Paste**.
- **Good to know:** pasting while typing in a text box still pastes text as usual. Big pictures are made screen-sized, see-through PNGs keep their transparency, and ↶ Undo removes a pasted picture.

## Backgrounds and sounds

☰ → *Background* has four tabs, and opens on the one your screen is using:

- **📚 Library:** ready-made pictures and moving loops that come with the site, shown as preview tiles (hover with a mouse to see a moving one play). They also work in share links, because they live on the site.
- **✨ Animated** (drawn live, no downloads): aurora, calm waves, bubbles, snow, rain, night sky, fireflies, clouds, autumn leaves and confetti, with a speed slider. They pause when the screen is paused or the tab is hidden, and stay still if the computer asks for reduced motion.
- **🎨 Colours:** plain colours and gradients.
- **📁 Your own:** a picture or looping video from the computer, a link, or a slideshow.
  - **Video or web link:** upload a looping video, or paste a link to a video file, a picture or animated GIF, a YouTube video (plays muted and looping), or a Google Drive file shared as "Anyone with the link".
  - **Slideshow:** choose a folder of pictures (and short videos); fade or slow-zoom, every 5 s–5 min, shuffled.
- **Darken** slider for any moving background, so widgets stand out.
- Uploaded pictures, videos and slideshows stay on the computer and go in `.classroom` files, not share links.

**🎧 Sound library** (in the Audio widget): sounds that come with the site, for quiet background sound in class. They loop with no gap and fade gently in and out.

**Adding to the libraries:** drop pictures (jpg, png, webp, gif) or short looping videos (mp4, webm) into the `backgrounds/` folder on GitHub, or sound files (mp3, m4a, ogg, wav) into `sounds/`. The *Background library* GitHub Action then makes the previews and the lists (`backgrounds/index.json`, `sounds/index.json`) automatically. Short seamless loops work best.

## ⭐ My widgets (a library of your own widgets)

- **Save one:** set a widget up how you like it (size, spot, settings, content, colour theme, text size), then tap **☆** on its title bar. A message confirms it, with **Rename** and **Undo**.
- **Use one:** tap **⭐ My widgets** at the start of the widget bar, then tap a saved widget. It's added to this screen at the size and spot it was saved with (kept inside the edges on a smaller screen, and nudged if an identical one is already there).
- **Saved widgets start fresh:**
  - **Kept:** settings and content.
  - **Reset:** timers back to their set time, stopwatches at zero, picked names, scores, dice and poll votes cleared, lists unticked, Now/Next back to the first item.
- **Each one has a small picture** of how it looked when saved, plus its name (e.g. "Timer · 10:00 · Morning task"). **✎** renames it, and **✕** removes it (with Undo).
- **It's a copy:** changing a placed widget doesn't change the saved one, and the other way round.
- **One library for all your screens,** kept in this browser. **Back up everything** includes it, and adding that backup file brings missing ones back. It isn't included in links or Save to a file.
- **Pictures, sounds and clips** a saved widget uses are kept, even if no screen uses them any more.

## Widget settings, themes and text size

- **⚙ settings:** tapping ⚙ opens a widget's settings in a panel at the side of the screen (on the left if the widget is on the right), so you can watch the widget change as you adjust it.
  - Every widget's settings follow the same order: what it shows, how it behaves, sounds, then how it looks. Theme and text size are always last.
  - Tap outside the panel, press Esc, or tap Done to close it. On phones the panel comes up from the bottom.
- **Colour themes:** every widget can be **Light, Dark, Transparent · white, Transparent · dark, Transparent · white edge, Transparent · dark edge, Blue or Yellow**. The edge ones add a thin outline to set a transparent widget apart.
  - Green, Pink and Purple are no longer offered, but widgets already using them keep their colour.
  - A **drop shadow** on words and pictures helps the transparent themes over a photo background.
- **Text size:** each widget's ⚙ has a slider (50–250%).
- **For every widget at once:** ☰ → *Widget theme & text size* sets the theme and text size for every widget on the screen, and for new ones. Settings panels always stay light, so they're easy to read.

## Phones and tablets

Tablets use the same free layout as the smartboard. On phones (and short landscape screens), widgets stack in a scrolling column, two side by side in landscape, in the same reading order as the big layout. The saved layout doesn't change, so a screen set up on a phone still looks right on the smartboard, and vice versa. Moving and resizing are switched off in this view.

## Smartboard tips

- **Multi-touch:** several children can use different widgets at the same time (e.g. a calculator each, or dragging two widgets at once). Every finger is tracked separately.
- **Full screen:** press **F** or tap ⛶.
- **☰ → Options:**
  - *Hide widget title bars* gives a clean display; tap a widget to show its controls again.
  - *Large buttons* makes the controls bigger.
  - *Snap widgets to a grid* lines them up neatly.
- **🔒** locks a widget so it can't be moved by accident. **▾** above the widget bar hides the bar.
- **Sound:** tap the screen once after opening the page. Browsers only allow sound after a tap, and alarms need it.
- **Alarms in the background:** alarms and timers keep ringing when another tab is in front. Their sounds are booked ahead on the audio clock, and a background worker keeps the countdown running. See ☰ → *Keep alarms ringing* to stop the browser putting the tab to sleep.
- **Offline:** after the first visit the site works offline, so it keeps running when the school wifi drops. Videos still need the internet.
- **YouTube on a phone** stops when the phone's screen turns off. YouTube blocks background play in browsers unless you have YouTube Premium. Sounds from the Audio widget and the sound library keep playing.

## Notes and ways to help

- **First visit:** a short 👋 welcome.
- **After an update:** a once-off ✨ What's new note for people already using the site.
- **Last week of term:** a once-a-term 🎉 thank-you with a backup reminder.
- **Ways to help:** each note shows ways to help: tell a colleague, tell me how you use it, suggest an idea or report a bug, send a kind word, or shout a coffee. They can all be opened again from ☰ → Help, along with 💛 *Ways to help*.
- **Owner settings** are in `js/config.js`:
  - the coffee link and a feedback form link;
  - `storyEmail`: where teachers send how they use the site. It adds **📸 Tell me how you use it** to Ways to help. That button opens a ready-made email with prompts (year level, favourite widgets, a moment it helped), a reminder to leave out students' names and faces, and a choice of permission: *ask me first*, *without my name*, or *with my first name and school*. There are also buttons to copy the address and questions (for school computers with no mail app) and to save a picture of the screen to attach.
  - an optional coffee goal (*3 of 10 coffees towards…*);
  - a thank-you list.

  Anything left empty doesn't show. Add a new entry to `WHATS_NEW` there for each update worth announcing.

## Ideas for later

- **micro:bit and Arduino input** (Web Serial): let a button or sensor start a timer, pick a name or drive a meter.
- **Draw over everything:** an annotation layer across the whole screen, so you can circle or underline anything — a widget, a name, the background — not just inside a whiteboard.
- **Think-Pair-Share** and other learning-structure widgets.
- **Maths tools:** number line, ten-frame, 120 chart.
- **Exit tickets.**
- **Other languages,** starting with the words students read on the board (work symbols, traffic light, Now / Next, timer labels).
- **Keep the screen on** while a YouTube video plays on a phone or tablet (screen wake lock).
- **If background alarms still prove unreliable:** an optional "keep tab awake" switch that plays an inaudible tone while a timer runs or an alarm is due. Browsers don't slow down or put to sleep a tab that is playing sound, but a speaker icon shows on the tab.
- **Recording data** (tallies, points, timings and noise levels saved to a log with a CSV download) was removed in mcs-v55. [docs/removed-recording-data.md](docs/removed-recording-data.md) explains how it worked, where to find the code, and what to do differently if it comes back. If a browser still has old records, it offers once to download them and delete them.

## Turning on GitHub Pages (for your own copy)

1. In the repository on GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under *Build and deployment*, set **Source: Deploy from a branch**, **Branch: `main`**, folder **`/ (root)`**, then **Save**.
3. After a minute the site will be live at `https://<your-user-name>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.

## For developers

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules, with no build step. Open `index.html` through any static server (e.g. `python3 -m http.server`) and edit the files directly.

```
index.html            page shell
css/app.css           all styles
js/main.js            app: screens, menu, groups, dock, saving and sharing dialogs, undo, tabs
js/board.js           drag / resize / z-order (Pointer Events: mouse, pen and touch)
js/storage.js         IndexedDB (screens, uploaded files, settings)
js/share.js           share links (deflate + base64url in the #hash) and .classroom files
js/zip.js             tiny zip reader/writer for .classroom files
js/classes.js         class lists, keep-apart pairs, unique names
js/groups.js          screen groups (name, colour, open/closed), kept with files and backups
js/templates.js       ready-made screens and gradient backgrounds
js/background.js      moving backgrounds: animations, videos, YouTube, Drive, slideshows
js/library.js         the backgrounds/ and sounds/ libraries (index.json) and gapless looping
js/themes.js          widget colour themes
js/termdates.js       school term dates by state/territory and New Zealand
js/sound.js           sound effects made with Web Audio (no audio files)
js/ticker.js          a clock that keeps ticking in a background tab
js/videoencode.js     whiteboard recordings → MP4 (WebCodecs)
js/screenshot.js      ☰ → Save a picture (tab capture → JPG, or a one-page PDF written by hand)
js/backup.js          backup file of everything, last-backed-up status, persistent storage, ask once
js/welcome.js         welcome, what's new, end of term, ways to help
js/config.js          coffee / feedback links, coffee goal, thank-you list, What's new entries
js/tabs.js            tabs tell each other which screen they use; opening one claims it (BroadcastChannel)
js/help.js            "Keep alarms ringing" help
js/oldrecords.js      one-time offer to download and delete records from the removed Data feature
js/util.js            small shared helpers
js/widgets/*.js       one file per widget; register new ones in js/widgets/index.js
js/vendor/            QR code generator, MP4 muxer
tools/                scripts the GitHub Action runs to build the background and sound lists
sw.js                 offline cache. Bump VERSION when files change
```

To add a widget, copy an existing widget file. Export an object with `type`, `title`, `icon`, `group`, `size`, `defaultState()` and `mount(ctx)`. Then add it to `js/widgets/index.js` (in the right category) and to the file list in `sw.js`. Put anything that holds student names in `privateKeys` so share links can leave it out.
